Professional Profile

Dr. Ananya Sharma is a distinguished practitioner of Ayurvedic medicine with a specialized focus on Internal Medicine (Kayachikitsa) and Ayurvedic pharmacology (Dravyaguna). With over a decade of clinical experience, she bridges the gap between traditional botanical wisdom and modern diagnostic precision.

Her approach centers on the fundamental principles of Prakriti (individual constitution) and Vikriti (current imbalance). She has successfully treated chronic metabolic disorders, respiratory conditions, and autoimmune diseases using customized herbal formulations and Panchakarma therapies. Dr. Sharma is a frequent contributor to international wellness journals and has pioneered research in integrating Ayurvedic nutrition into modern hospital settings.
